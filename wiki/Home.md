# Welcome to SportApp Wiki

Welcome to the official documentation for **SportApp** - a comprehensive platform for sports field booking, team matchmaking, and real-time communication.

## 🚀 Overview

SportApp aims to connect sports enthusiasts by providing an easy-to-use platform to:
- **Book Venues:** Search for available sports fields, view details via maps, and book them securely.
- **Matchmaking (Tìm đối):** Create posts to find teammates or opponents. Automatically generate group chat rooms upon accepting requests.
- **Real-time Chat:** Seamlessly communicate with other users or team members with instant notifications.
- **AI Chatbot:** Get quick answers to FAQs and receive intelligent venue suggestions via our built-in assistant.

## 🛠 Tech Stack Highlights
- **Frontend:** Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet Maps.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, Prisma ORM, BullMQ.
- **Realtime:** Socket.io, Redis.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL.
